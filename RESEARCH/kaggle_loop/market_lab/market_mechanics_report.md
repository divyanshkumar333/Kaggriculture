# Market Laboratory: Exact Queue Ordering & Price Mechanics

## 1. Executive Summary

Using the official, byte-for-byte unmodified `kaggriculture.py` engine (`bc8a54879ef02c7ea64b8b333d6a976f0ea65c4949149d01f463f23bccee653e`), we conducted controlled laboratory experiments to determine the exact mathematical edge generated by queue slot placement, chunking, and commodity prioritization.

## 2. Controlled Laboratory Findings

### Experiment 1: Premium Lead vs Bulk Lead (Slot Contention)
- **Setup**: Both players hold 10 Strawberry and 50 Wheat.
  - **Player 0**: Slot 0 = `SELL STRAWBERRY 10`, Slot 1 = `SELL WHEAT 50`
  - **Player 1**: Slot 0 = `SELL WHEAT 50`, Slot 1 = `SELL STRAWBERRY 10`
- **Result**:
  - Player 0 Revenue: **$2,179.00**
  - Player 1 Revenue: **$2,048.00**
  - **Net Margin**: **+$131.00 (+6.4%)** in a single turn on only 10 strawberries.
- **Mechanism**: The engine processes orders slot-by-slot (`i in range(maxMarketOrdersPerTurn)`). In Slot 0, Player 0's strawberries sell at the un-crashed market rate. By the time Slot 1 executes, market inventory has absorbed supply, forcing Player 1's strawberries to sell into the depressed price curve.

### Experiment 2: Chunking Hazard (Single Order vs Split Order)
- **Setup**: Both players sell 10 Strawberry in the same turn.
  - **Player 0**: 1 single order: `[["SELL", "STRAWBERRY", 10]]`
  - **Player 1**: 2 split orders: `[["SELL", "STRAWBERRY", 5], ["SELL", "STRAWBERRY", 5]]`
- **Result**:
  - Player 0 Revenue: **$1,046.00**
  - Player 1 Revenue: **$997.00**
  - **Chunking Penalty**: **-$49.00 (-4.7%)**
- **Mechanism**: In Slot 0, the engine executes lockstep until Player 1's 5 units are exhausted. Because Player 0's order has remaining capacity (5 units), Player 0 continues selling units 6–10 in Slot 0. When Slot 1 begins, Player 1's second chunk of 5 units sells at the depressed inventory level left by Player 0. **Chunking sales across multiple order slots is actively destructive.**

### Experiment 3: Duplicate Commodity Gating (The V104 Anomaly)
- **Setup**: Player 1 has multiple orders of wheat before strawberry (e.g. from upstream fragmented inventory drops):
  - **Player 0 (Sorted)**: `[["SELL", "STRAWBERRY", 10], ["SELL", "WHEAT", 10]]`
  - **Player 1 (Gated / Unsorted)**: `[["SELL", "WHEAT", 5], ["SELL", "WHEAT", 5], ["SELL", "STRAWBERRY", 10]]`
- **Result**:
  - Player 0 Revenue: **$1,344.00**
  - Player 1 Revenue: **$1,158.00**
  - **Gating Penalty**: **-$186.00 (-13.8%)** in ONE turn.
- **Root Cause**: The 2945 farm's `_r37_reorder_sales` layer contained:
  ```python
  if len({o[1] for o in block}) == len(block):
      orders[start:end] = sorted(block, key=lambda o: _r37_quote_priority(observation, o, stock), reverse=True)
  ```
  When multiple orders of the same commodity were present in a contiguous sell block, the condition evaluated to `False`, completely disabling the sort. V104 eliminated this gate.

## 4. Key Strategic Directives for Agent Design

1. **Optimal Sell Queue Ranking**:
   - `STRAWBERRY` > `MILK` > `WOOL` > `MELON` > `TOMATO` > `CARROT` > `EGG` > `WHEAT` > `FERTILIZER`.
   - Selling Strawberry before Melon captures Strawberry at $111 while Melon still fetches $242+. Inverting this burns Strawberry down to $38 while Melon only gains ~$8.
2. **Never Chunk Orders of the Same Commodity**:
   - Merge all available volume of item $X$ into a single `["SELL", X, total_qty]` order in Slot 0 or 1.
   - Do NOT submit `[SELL, X, 5]` followed by `[SELL, X, 5]`.
3. **Always Strip Gating Conditions on Reordering**:
   - Any check that skips sorting when duplicate commodities exist forfeits $100–$200+ per turn in mixed sale waves.
