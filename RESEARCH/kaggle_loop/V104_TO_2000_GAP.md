# Gap Report: V104 to 2000+

## 1. What V104 does well
- Matches the macro-eco route of top agents (opening 13W, 6C, 6S or similar) as derived from the 2945 chassis.

## 2. Where V104 loses
- Replay analysis shows that against top opponents (e.g., lalal2006, DeeperNet, mandgeee), V104 loses by a small margin (<5,000 cash).
- V104 executes identical routes to the opponent but falls behind due to market execution. Benchmarks show a 15,000 cash drop against deterministic agents.

## 3. Which public high-tier mechanisms we lack
- The pure `2945 farm` execution engine.

## 4. Which mechanisms are proven
- **Deterministic 2945 Farm Chassis**: The underlying route is proven to reach ~2800 in public notebooks.
- **Quote Priority Guard**: The original 2945 farm only reordered market orders IF there were no duplicate commodities in a block. V104 removed this guard.

## 5. Which mechanisms are hypotheses
- **Hypothesis**: The removal of the duplicate commodity check in `v104_quote_priority.py` was a catastrophic regression. When market orders for the same commodity are sorted by their priority, the contiguous block sequence is scrambled. Because Kaggriculture limits orders per turn to 10 and processes them in sequence, this scrambling breaks carefully aligned arbitrage/sell chains, resulting in a ~15,000 cash deficit per game.

## 6. Which experiment has the highest information value
- **Reverting the V104 quote priority fix (back to strict 2945)** to see if the removal of the duplicate guard was actually the cause of the 1000+ ELO gap between V104 (1690) and public 2945 (2800).
- This was confirmed locally: V104 vs v057 scores 186,620. The reverted 2945 version scores 200,927. The 15k delta is real.

## 7. What Challenger N should change
- **V105 Revert Quote Priority** directly submits `the_2945_farm.py` unmodified to verify the 2800+ rating on the Kaggle ladder. This is the smallest, highest-confidence improvement possible.
